## Huntly - East Rd

A standout APZ treatment protecting the eastern edge of Huntly. While legacy Risk 1 modelling identifies only a single modelled home saved, Risk 2.0 estimates the burn could save more than 2,600 homes during future bushfire events, highlighting the significant residential growth not captured by older frameworks. The burn also contributes to the protection of 24 homes within 100 m and 687 homes within 1 km, making it one of the strongest community protection opportunities in the program and a clear priority for delivery.


## Kangaroo Flat - Tipperary Gully

One of the most strategically important APZ treatments in the region, located within the urban footprint of Kangaroo Flat between the railway corridor and Allingham Street. While traditional modelling underestimates its importance, Risk 2.0 estimates the treatment could save more than 2,600 homes during future bushfire events. With 52 homes within 100 m and over 1,400 homes within 1 km, alongside nearby commercial and industrial assets, this burn delivers exceptional community protection outcomes and should be considered a priority for implementation.


## Barkers Creek - Blakeley Rd

A high-value APZ treatment northwest of Castlemaine delivering strong risk reduction outcomes under both operational and modelling lenses. Risk 2.0 estimates the burn could save more than 1,600 homes during future bushfire events, substantially exceeding the benefits identified by traditional modelling. The treatment also contributes to the protection of 8 homes within 100 m and 277 homes within 1 km while reducing risk in an area with a demonstrated history of significant fire activity. Very close to the Ravenswood - Fogarty's Gap fire scar, burning in this catchment is a priority. 


## Newstead - Pyrenees Hwy
<br>
A strategically important roadside treatment that functions similarly to a Strategic Fuel Break, supporting suppression activities, firefighter access and community egress along the Pyrenees Highway corridor. While recent fuel history suppresses short-term Risk 1 benefits, Risk 2.0 estimates the burn could save more than 1,500 homes during future bushfire events, making it one of the strongest long-term risk reduction opportunities in the program. Its value extends well beyond the treatment footprint through improved suppression capability and protection of nearby communities and critical transport infrastructure.

## Wedderburn - Daisy Ln

One of the highest-performing APZ treatments in the program and the fifth-highest priority burn for delivery this year. The treatment is modelled to save 226 homes under Risk 1 and 692 homes under Risk 2.0, demonstrating consistently strong performance regardless of modelling methodology. In addition to delivering significant risk reduction outcomes, the burn contributes to the protection of almost 300 homes within 1 km, making it an outstanding investment in community safety.


## Huntly - Viola Rd

This grassland treatment presents an excellent opportunity to secure both operational and community protection outcomes. Risk 2.0 estimates the burn could save more than 600 homes during future bushfire events, highlighting risk reduction benefits that are not immediately obvious on the ground. Combined with its role in supporting CFA training, operational readiness and ongoing fuel management, the burn offers a highly efficient way to deliver multiple strategic objectives through a single treatment.


## Avoca - Boundary Rd

Despite limited modelled risk reduction under current frameworks, this remains a highly worthwhile APZ west of Avoca. The burn contributes directly to the protection of 22 homes within 100 m and 579 homes within 1 km, providing substantial asset protection benefits for the township edge. Its value is likely underrepresented by current modelling, with the treatment delivering important opportunities to reduce fire intensity, support suppression activities and improve protection for a large number of nearby residents.


## Tarnagulla - Golf Course

Located immediately northwest of Tarnagulla, this burn provides meaningful township protection while reducing bushfire risk across the surrounding landscape. Risk 2.0 estimates the treatment could save almost 500 homes during future bushfire events, demonstrating strong risk reduction performance. Combined with protection benefits for 151 homes within 1 km, the burn delivers a compelling return on investment and helps reduce the likelihood of damaging fire behaviour impacting the township.


## Rushworth - Perry Rd

A strong BMZ treatment in a high-risk landscape north of Rushworth. The burn is modelled to save 35 homes under Risk 1 and more than 450 homes under Risk 2.0, highlighting substantial benefits not fully captured by legacy modelling. In addition to delivering meaningful landscape-scale risk reduction, the treatment contributes to the protection of 341 homes within the surrounding area and supports broader efforts to reduce the likelihood of large, high-intensity bushfire impacting the township and adjacent communities.


## Talbot - Amherst BR

A small but strategically important APZ west of Talbot that delivers benefits well beyond its modest footprint. Risk 2.0 estimates the burn could save almost 400 homes during future bushfire events, demonstrating that the treatment influences risk across a much broader area than might be expected. The burn also contributes to the protection of nearby assets and strengthens the fuel management network supporting Talbot and surrounding rural communities.


## Heathcote - Pannells Tk

The sixth-highest ranked burn in Year 1 of the JFMP and fifteenth overall, underscoring its significance within the optimisation process. The treatment is modelled to save 214 homes under Risk 1 and 343 homes under Risk 2.0, delivering strong and consistent risk reduction outcomes under both frameworks. While only 45 homes are located within 1 km of the burn, its strategic value lies in reducing bushfire risk across the broader Heathcote and Argyle landscape, helping to prevent damaging fire runs and improving protection of life and property.


## St Arnaud - Silvermines Rd Complex

Comprising three treatment areas embedded within the residential edge of St Arnaud, this burn delivers direct and highly visible community protection outcomes. Risk 2.0 estimates the treatment could save more than 330 homes during future bushfire events, while the burn also contributes to the protection of 18 homes within 100 m and 476 homes within 1 km. Its location immediately adjacent to residential development makes it an important township-edge APZ and a valuable component of the broader fuel management network protecting St Arnaud.
